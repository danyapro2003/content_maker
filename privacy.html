Privacy Policy

This policy covers the Content Clipper Windows application and its project website.

Data stored on your computer

The application stores its settings, project files, generated clips and processing logs locally. The publishing queue stores connected account names and identifiers, selected clip paths, titles, captions, scheduled times and upload history. Publishing credentials, including access and refresh tokens, are encrypted using Windows Data Protection for the current user.

Connected accounts and video uploads

TikTok Login Kit asks for your permission in a browser. Content Clipper uses TikTok's basic profile permission to read the account identifier and display name so you can select the correct account. The video upload permission lets the app send your chosen MP4 to the connected account's TikTok inbox for review and publication. TikTok handles the uploaded video and your activity on its platform under its own privacy policy.

When you connect a different publishing service, the app sends the files, links or post details needed for the action you schedule to that service.

Optional AI services

If you configure an external AI provider, features such as clip selection or translation can send transcripts, text or other analysis input to that provider. The provider's own data terms apply. The project website does not process those requests.

Retention and controls

Local settings, files and queue history remain on your computer until you remove them. Disconnecting a publishing account removes its saved access from Content Clipper. You can separately revoke app access in the connected platform's settings and manage uploaded content on that platform.

Website and contact

This static site has no account, form or analytics script. It is hosted by GitHub Pages, which may log visitor IP addresses for security. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

For privacy questions, contact the maintainer through GitHub Issues. Issues are public. Keep account credentials and private media out of reports.
