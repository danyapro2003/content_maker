TikTok setup

Connect your profile, choose a clip and finish publishing from TikTok.

Connect your profile

In Content Clipper, open the scheduling tab, choose Connect account and select TikTok. Enter the client key and client secret from your TikTok developer app. Content Clipper opens TikTok in your browser, where you choose a profile and approve access. Repeat for each profile you want to connect.

Schedule an upload

Choose a finished MP4, select a connected TikTok account and set the time. Keep Content Clipper running for scheduled uploads. If it is closed at the scheduled time, it resumes the queue the next time you open it.

Publish the draft

When TikTok finishes processing, open the upload notification from your TikTok inbox. Review the video and caption, choose visibility and publish it from TikTok.

Disconnect a profile

Cancel pending uploads for the account, then select it and choose Disconnect in Content Clipper. You can also revoke authorization in TikTok under Settings and privacy, Security and permissions, Apps and services.
