Terms of Service

These terms apply to the Content Clipper Windows application and its project website.

Use of the application

Content Clipper helps you prepare video clips and schedule uploads to accounts you connect. Use accounts you are authorized to manage. Check the destination, caption and video before scheduling an upload.

Content and rights

You are responsible for the content you import, edit, upload or publish. Only use material you have the rights or permission to use. You retain any rights you hold in your videos and captions.

Third-party platforms

Connected services apply their own terms, content rules and account limits. Your platform controls whether it accepts or publishes an upload. For TikTok, Content Clipper transfers the video for review and you complete publication in TikTok.

Scheduling

Scheduled uploads depend on your computer, network, account authorization and platform availability. Check the publishing queue and destination account for the status of an upload.

Support

For questions about the application or these terms, contact the maintainer through GitHub Issues.
